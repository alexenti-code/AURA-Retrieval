# Matryoshka Public Research Scope

## Publicly disclosed

This repository publicly discloses the conceptual architecture of Matryoshka Continuity Weights, its terms, its core distinctions and a high-level evaluation direction.

It also may contain limited experimental code intended to demonstrate or falsify selected claims under controlled conditions.

## Boundary with the deployment environment

The sandbox, documents, APIs, access rights and other externally available
capabilities belong to the deployment environment and are separate from
Matryoshka. The environment determines which objects and actions exist;
Matryoshka preserves and self-organizes the experience lived within it.
Matryoshka itself defines no goals, permissions, policies, tool access or
executive control. See [BOUNDARY.md](BOUNDARY.md).

## Not publicly disclosed

The following are intentionally outside the public disclosure unless explicitly published in a later signed release:

- exact mechanisms for generating parameter updates;
- learning objectives, loss functions and update schedules;
- topologies, tensor layouts and layer-placement strategies;
- mechanisms for temporal binding, interference mitigation and memory consolidation;
- proprietary datasets, private evaluation records and commercial deployment data;
- AURA production infrastructure, credentials, integrations, client information and operational policies;
- implementation details that may constitute trade secrets or future patent subject matter.

## Archive and evidence

Primary documents, source records, hashes, digital signatures and timestamps may be retained separately as independent records of reality. Their existence does not make the public disclosure a retrieval-based architecture.

## Versioning policy

Every public release is a dated and versioned disclosure of exactly the files included in that release. Later text must identify whether it:

- clarifies the original proposition;
- adds experimental evidence;
- changes terminology; or
- discloses a new technical mechanism.

No document in this repository grants a licence to undisclosed implementation methods or confidential AURA systems.
